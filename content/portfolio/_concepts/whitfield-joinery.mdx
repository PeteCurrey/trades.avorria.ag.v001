## The Challenge

Whitfield Joinery is a craft-first business. Their work is visually stunning but they were stuck in the "low-value" carpentry market — hanging doors and fitting skirting — because their website looked like a basic handyman service.

## The Approach

We built a high-end "Editorial" portfolio.

- **Magazine-Style Layout**: Used asymmetric grids and prestige typography (Syne & Inter) to mimic luxury interior design magazines.
- **Material Showcase**: Detailed pages explaining their use of sustainably sourced oak, walnut, and traditional joinery techniques.
- **Instagram Live Feed**: Integrated their social feed to show "work in progress" and the human side of the workshop.
- **Heritage SEO**: Targeted "bespoke joinery York" and "heritage window repair" to attract owners of York's historic townhouses.

## The Results

- **Shifted market position**: They now exclusively take on bespoke projects, with no more "handyman" enquiries.
- **Average project value rose from £800 to £4,500**.
- **18k new followers across channels** as the website became a hub for their visual content.
- **Waitlist created**: Now booking projects 6 months in advance.
