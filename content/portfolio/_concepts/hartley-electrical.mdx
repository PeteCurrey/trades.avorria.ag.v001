## The Challenge

Hartley Electrical had a strong local reputation built over 12 years, but their website — built on a generic template back in 2018 — was holding the business back. It loaded slowly on mobile, didn't display NICEIC accreditation prominently, and had no dedicated emergency callout page.

The result: customers who searched "emergency electrician Leeds" at 11pm with a tripped consumer unit bounced to competitors with cleaner, faster sites. The owner estimated they were losing 8–12 high-value emergency jobs per month.

## The Approach

We built a 9-page bespoke website from the ground up, designed around the search behaviour of Leeds homeowners. Key decisions:

- **NICEIC badge above the fold** on every page, with a direct link to their public NICEIC register entry to build instant trust
- **Dedicated emergency callout page** with massive click-to-call buttons, optimised for "emergency electrician Leeds" and surrounding area searches
- **EV charger installation landing page** targeting the rapidly growing OZEV-grant market — high-value enquiries with strong commercial intent
- **EICR testing page** specifically for landlords, with a streamlined enquiry form for multi-property bookings
- **Local SEO architecture** with dedicated landing content for LS6, LS7, LS8, LS17, and surrounding postcodes
- **TradeDesk integration** so jobs booked via the website flow straight into the management platform

## The Results

Within 90 days of launch:

- **47% increase in monthly enquiries** through the website contact form
- **First-page Google ranking** for "emergency electrician Leeds" and "EV charger installer Leeds"
- **3.2× increase in EV charger enquiries** from the dedicated landing page
- **Bounce rate dropped from 68% to 31%** thanks to the faster mobile load times
- **Customer satisfaction score** of 5.0 across all platforms

The website now functions as the primary lead source for Hartley Electrical, replacing dependency on Checkatrade and word-of-mouth referrals as the main growth driver.
