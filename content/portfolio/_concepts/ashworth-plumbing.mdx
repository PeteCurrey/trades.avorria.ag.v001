## The Challenge

Ashworth Plumbing relied heavily on Google LSA (Local Services Ads) but found the cost-per-lead was eating into their margins for smaller repair jobs. Their existing "one-page" website was barely indexed by Google and didn't provide enough information to convert higher-value boiler installation leads.

## The Approach

We developed a high-performance site focused on two distinct user journeys: the "Panic Searcher" (emergency leaks) and the "Planned Researcher" (boiler upgrades).

- **Ultra-fast Mobile Speed**: Optimised for 2G/3G connections to ensure the site loads instantly for users in distress.
- **Gas Safe Prominence**: Displayed their registration number in the header and footer to build immediate authority.
- **Online Booking Widget**: Integrated an annual service booking calendar to build a recurring revenue stream.
- **Landlord Portal**: A dedicated page for local letting agents to book multiple CP12 inspections simultaneously.

## The Results

- **62% increase in organic emergency callout bookings** within the first quarter.
- **Significant reduction in Google Ads spend** as organic local rankings improved for "plumber Derby".
- **Average order value increased by 22%** as the site started attracting more full-system installation enquiries.
- **98% of customers** cited the professional website as a key factor in their decision to hire Ashworth.
