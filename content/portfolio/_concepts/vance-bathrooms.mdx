## The Challenge

Vance Bathrooms were expert installers but their sales cycle was too slow. They were spending days on quotes only for clients to go with cheaper "all-in-one" retailers. They needed to position themselves as the "boutique alternative" to the big chains.

## The Approach

We built a luxury "Virtual Showroom".

- **Immersive Galleries**: Used 4K photography and video walkthroughs of finished bathrooms to convey the "luxury feel" that photos alone miss.
- **Fixture Guides**: Educated clients on the difference between high-end brassware and cheap alternatives.
- **Booking Fee System**: Integrated a small "design fee" booking system to qualify leads — ensuring they only spend time on serious prospects.
- **Stockport Expansion**: Targeted affluent areas like Bramhall and Hazel Grove with tailored landing pages.

## The Results

- **£22,000 increase in average monthly revenue** within 5 months.
- **Quote-to-Hire ratio improved by 65%** as the "design fee" filtered out time-wasters.
- **Project value doubled**: Clients were now requesting premium fixtures and underfloor heating by name.
- **Featured in "Manchester Interiors"** magazine as a top local installer.
