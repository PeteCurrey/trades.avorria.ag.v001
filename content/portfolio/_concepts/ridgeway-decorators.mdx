## The Challenge

Ridgeway Decorators were experts in their craft but were often outbid by lower-quality competitors because they couldn't justify their premium rates to potential clients who just saw "another painter".

## The Approach

We built a site that focused on "The Difference".

- **Macro Portfolio**: High-resolution close-ups of cutting, masking, and finish quality to show the difference between a £200 room and an £800 room.
- **Commercial Landing Page**: Specifically targeted estate agents and property managers with a "hassle-free" service pitch.
- **Spray Finishing Feature**: Highlighted their use of Airless Spray technology for kitchen resprays — a high-margin, high-demand service.
- **Colour Consultation Tool**: Integrated a tool to help clients pick palettes, positioning them as designers, not just labourers.

## The Results

- **2.8× increase in commercial enquiries** from local property management firms.
- **Secured a 3-year contract** for a major Leeds hotel chain.
- **Reduced quote-to-job time** as the website did 80% of the "selling" before the first visit.
- **92% of residential clients** opted for the "Full Designer" package rather than basic painting.
