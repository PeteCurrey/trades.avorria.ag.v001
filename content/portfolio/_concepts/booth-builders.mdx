## The Challenge

Booth Builders had a backlog of incredible work but no way to showcase it. They were using WhatsApp to send photos to potential clients, which felt unprofessional for £100k+ projects. They needed a "digital showroom" that reflected the precision of their builds.

## The Approach

We built a cinematic portfolio experience.

- **High-Resolution Galleries**: Implemented lazy-loaded, full-screen project galleries with "Before & After" sliders.
- **Process Transparency**: A dedicated page explaining the 6 stages from initial quote to final sign-off.
- **Testimonial Integration**: Video testimonials from past clients embedded directly into relevant project pages.
- **Project Location SEO**: Created landing pages for Sheffield's high-value areas like Ecclesall and Dore.

## The Results

- **210% increase in portfolio page engagement** time.
- **Successfully landed three £80k+ extensions** in the first 4 months directly attributed to the new site.
- **Referral-to-Lead conversion rate improved by 45%** as past clients now had a professional link to share.
- **Streamlined sales process**: The owner now uses the website as a visual aid during site visits.
