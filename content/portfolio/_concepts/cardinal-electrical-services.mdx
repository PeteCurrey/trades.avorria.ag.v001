## The Challenge

Cardinal Electrical was a successful business but they were "chasing work" every month. They had high turnover but low predictability. They needed their website to win them high-value, recurring service contracts from Birmingham's industrial and commercial sectors.

## The Approach

We built a "B2B Authority" platform.

- **Contractor Comparison Page**: A detailed table showing why their ECA-accredited status and £10m insurance coverage make them the safe choice for large firms.
- **Client Portal Concept**: Showcased how they manage compliance documentation for their clients (integrated with TradeDesk).
- **Industrial SEO**: Targeted "industrial electrician Birmingham" and "office data cabling Black Country".
- **Case Study Focus**: Created long-form case studies of warehouse fit-outs and retail park maintenance projects.

## The Results

- **12 new annual service contracts** signed within 6 months, creating a stable recurring revenue floor.
- **Became the "Preferred Contractor"** for three major Birmingham property management groups.
- **250% increase in EICR testing volume** as the site automated the lead-capture process for multi-site landlords.
- **Average contract length increased** as the professional site proved their long-term stability.
