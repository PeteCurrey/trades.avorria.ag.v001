## The Challenge

Pennine Heating Solutions provided premium engineering work but their digital presence felt "budget". They were struggling to win higher-end installation projects because their portfolio didn't reflect the complexity and quality of their stone-property central heating work in western Sheffield.

## The Approach

We pivoted the brand from "generic gas man" to "heating system architects".

- **Portfolio-First Design**: Implemented high-resolution case studies showing complex pipework and premium smart-control installations.
- **Grant Checker**: Added a simple tool for homeowners to check eligibility for energy-efficiency grants.
- **Manufacturer Partnerships**: Highlighted their status as Accredited Installers for Worcester Bosch and Vaillant.
- **Stone-Property Specialist SEO**: Targeted keywords related to period property heating, loft-conversion plumbing, and complex system design.

## The Results

- **£14,000 in new qualified installation pipeline** generated in the first 30 days.
- **Conversion rate on contact forms tripled** compared to the old site.
- **Awarded "Regional Installer of the Year"** by a major manufacturer, citing their professional digital presence as a benchmark for the trade.
- **Ranked #1 for "premium boiler installation Sheffield"** within 4 months.
