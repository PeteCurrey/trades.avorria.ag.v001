## The Challenge

Driveway installation is a crowded market with high competition. Apex Driveways was struggling to convert website traffic because their old site didn't address the #1 customer fear: "Will it crack in two years?".

## The Approach

We built a "Durability & Trust" engine.

- **Cross-Section Graphics**: Created custom illustrations showing the layers of sub-base and drainage they install — proving why they are better than "cowboy" competitors.
- **Resin Comparison Guide**: A detailed breakdown of Resin-Bound vs Resin-Bonded (a common point of confusion for customers).
- **Kerb Appeal Gallery**: Filterable portfolio showing different stone colours and border patterns to inspire homeowners.
- **Derbyshire Targeting**: Specific landing pages for Ashbourne and Belper to capture the high-end rural market.

## The Results

- **40% increase in website visitor-to-lead conversion rate**.
- **Significant decrease in price-shopping**: Customers were willing to pay their premium because they understood the value of the sub-base work.
- **Ranked #1 for "resin driveways Derby"** within 3 months.
- **Zero negative reviews** since launch, as the site set clear expectations for the build process.
