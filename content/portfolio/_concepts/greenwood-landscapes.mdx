## The Challenge

Landscaping is notoriously seasonal. Greenwood Landscapes had more work than they could handle in May, but their crew was idle in January. They needed a website that could drive "planning leads" during the off-season.

## The Approach

We built a seasonal-intelligent marketing site.

- **"Plan Your Summer" Campaign**: A winter-specific landing page offering design consultations and early-booking discounts.
- **Material Samples Gallery**: High-res photos of porcelain, sandstone, and composite decking options to help clients choose from home.
- **3D Design Showcase**: Highlighted their use of CAD/3D rendering to help clients visualise their garden before the first spade hits the ground.
- **Resin Driveway Landing Page**: Targeted a high-volume, year-round search term to balance seasonal fluctuations.

## The Results

- **85% increase in winter design bookings**, ensuring the team remained full-capacity through the off-season.
- **Project lead-time extended to 8 months**, allowing for better resource planning.
- **Conversion rate on driveways doubled** thanks to the detailed material comparison guides.
- **Average garden project value increased by 35%** as clients added more premium features during the design phase.
